Podium and sticky bar, round 2

From your staging test: more podium bars, a taller sticky bar that no longer shakes, and a phone bar that stays longer.

1Podium with more bars

Slightly narrower bars and more of them, arranged like a real podium: 1 in the middle, then 2 and 3 either side, then the runners-up. Gold, silver and bronze stay the stars; places 4 and beyond use a muted version of the kit's purple. Hover each card: the bars rise from the middle outwards.

Recommend 5 bars. It reads instantly as a leaderboard top five, the bars stay chunky enough for their numbers, and the rise from the middle outwards is a nice little ripple. Seven bars looks busier and the numbers get small, especially on phones.

2Desktop: taller, and no more shaking

Try it with your notched wheel: scroll slowly down and up across the point where the bar slims. It no longer shakes.

  • Taller: the bar is 76px at rest (was about 64) and 60px when slimmed (was 52), and the switch buttons are a little roomier.
  • Why it shook: slimming the bar made the page shorter, which pulled you back above the switch point, so it grew again, and flipped with every notch of your wheel.
  • The fix, two parts: when the bar slims, it adds the lost height back underneath, so the page never changes length; and it slims at one point but only grows back 40px higher up, so it can't flip at the edge.
3Phones: taller, and it stays longer

Scroll down steadily, then flick back up.

Scrolled down in one go: 0 px · hides at 200
  • Taller: 56px (was 46), with 44 × 40 icon buttons.
  • Stays longer: it never hides during the first screen of the page, and after that only once you've scrolled down steadily for about a third of a screen (200px on a phone). Small scrolls and pauses don't count.
  • Comes back: a flick up of about 24px brings it back, and it's always there at the top of the page.
  • These three numbers are easy to tune after you've tried them on your phone.
