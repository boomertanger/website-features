How slides get chosen

  • Automatic slides (Stream, Vote, Goal, Series, Top fans) fill themselves from site data and appear only when there is something to show.
  • Template slides (The hub, Merch drop) are written in the admin area and can be scheduled.
  • While you're live, the Stream slide still takes over.

Keep in mind

  • Five or six slides at a time is plenty; twelve would take almost 90 seconds to cycle.
  • Some slides depend on features that come later (Question Queue, points, contests).

The eight new slide types

VoteNew

A live community vote. Members tap to vote right on the slide; visitors are invited to join.

Data from: Schedule Planner votes

Game spotlightNew

One game with tags, your rating, how often you've streamed it and when you last played.

Data from: Game Vault

Clip of the weekNew

A 9:16 clip that feels at home for your TikTok audience, with reaction counts.

Data from: Stream Library clips

Top fansNew

The top three by points, plus where the viewer stands.

Data from: Points and badges

GiveawayNew

Shows the prize, drawing time and exactly what each member still needs to do to be eligible.

Data from: Contests and check-ins

GoalNew

Progress toward a follower goal on every platform, with the reward when you hit it.

Data from: Growth data collector

SeriesNew

Stats and a night-by-night grid for a running series like the Granny Gauntlet.

Data from: Stream Library

Ask BoomerNew

Top-voted questions with upvote buttons, answered on stream during breaks.

Data from: Question Queue

Recommendation: launch with Welcome, Stream, Vote and Goal

Those four work with the foundation, the Schedule Planner and the growth collector, and they tell a first-time visitor who you are, when you're on, how to take part and what the community is building toward. Add Series, Clip, Top fans, Giveaway and Ask Boomer as their features ship.
