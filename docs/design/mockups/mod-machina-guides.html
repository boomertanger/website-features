2 · /crew/hq · crew only

Crew HQ

Each crew member's home: this month's activity, upcoming duties with a Swap button, open seats that need help, the task board and progress to the next grade. The activity meter has two options; the toggle switches it.

★Recommendation: M2, the time card. One punched slot per duty makes "2 of 3" impossible to misread, the dashed fourth slot shows the On the Clock badge as the next thing to earn, and it fits the clock-in language the crew already uses. M1 (the kit's meter) is fine but reads like every other progress bar on the site.
