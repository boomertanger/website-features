1 · /schedule, crew view · Schedule Planner

Stream duty seats

The published week as the crew sees it. Each stream card shows its chats at a glance (lime = lead covered, gold = lead needed) and the Chat Game planned. Open a stream to see its seats and take one; seats your grade can't take are dimmed. Two ways to show the seats:

★Recommendation: S1, the seat map. Empty seats look like empty seats: a dashed chair with a plus is an invitation, and a gold one says "this is where we need you" before anyone reads a word. The roster table is denser but reads like a spreadsheet, and on phones it loses columns. The owner's planner (drag games into slots) is its own workstream; this is the crew's side of it.
2 · /live/deck · crew on duty

Mod Deck

The panel a crew member keeps open while Boomer is live: the stream clock, every chat's coverage, the chats themselves (Twitch and both YouTube chats can be embedded; TikTok links out), and a rail with your seat, the next Chat Game cue for your chat, quick lines to copy and, for Captains, drops and flags. On phones it becomes three tabs. Try Step away, and switch between the Room Lead and Captain views.

★Recommendation: D1, the chat wall. Early on, one YouTube lead covers both YouTube chats, and the only way to do that well is to see them side by side. D2 (your chat big, one beside it) is calmer and stays available as a toggle inside the Deck for anyone covering a single chat. Phones always get one chat at a time.
3 · /crew/games · crew

Chat Games pool

The four formats with what each needs and whether it's on cooldown, then the packs. Each crew member gets 3 votes a week; "I'll host this" counts three times and puts your face on the pack, so the pool rises on games someone will actually run. Played packs drop to the Used tab. Wardens and up see New pack.

4 · /live · members play, visitors watch

Playing Chat Games on /live

Under the stream: the check-in box (each chat has its own code, which is how Scream Off knows your chat), then the Play panel with tonight's games. Dead Air's clue 3 dropped in the YouTube vertical chat and shows here after a 10-minute countdown, so the fastest way to keep up is to go to that chat. Pick a suspect and a weapon to lock in. Two ways to show the case:

★Recommendation: C1, the evidence board. Pinned suspect cards and clue notes joined by red string are what a murder mystery should feel like, and it reuses the pinboard notes members already know from Warm Fuzzies. The countdown note on the board makes the cross-chat hunt obvious. C2 is tidier but turns the mystery into a form. On phones the strings drop away and the cards stack.
