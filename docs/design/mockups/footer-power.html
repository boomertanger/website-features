1 Follow only

Follow gets its hanging cables from its own node. Contact is restyled as matching glowing pills (same icon badges and colours) with no cables. Simplest, but the two halves feel like different systems.

2 Shared power (recommended)

One node in the middle, right where the chain hung, feeds cables to all seven badges: Contact on the left, Follow on the right. The whole footer becomes one wired-up machine, and both columns line up as equals. Show strikes lightning down that cable and the address decodes in.

3 Two circuits

Each column gets its own node and cables, symmetrical. Tidier on wide screens, a little busier overall. Show strikes the same lightning.

Recommendation: 2 Shared power

It solves the alignment question by making Contact and Follow one composition instead of two neighbours: same badge size, same row height, fed from one point. The lightning on Show gives Contact its own moment without competing with Follow's power-up. On phones each section gets its own short set of cables so nothing gets cramped.
