Recommendation

Stage tracker A, Assembly line. Your season rides a conveyor through eight machines, so at a glance you see what's built, what's on the line now and what's left. It's on-theme for a factory, works as a scrolling strip on phones, and every machine is a button, so it doubles as navigation. B is the most conventional and packs the most detail, but it looks like any settings page. C is the best picture of the season's shape, so it's kept inside Review as the "Plan map" (and the panel to the right of every stage could show it in a later version).

Season pass: today's jobs first (the reason to come back daily), then the week, then the story path. Locked Sub Club campaigns show for Fan Club members as an upgrade nudge. Crew missions only appear for mods and admins.

Proposed kit pieces: .bt-stepper (the assembly line, with the rail as its compact form), .bt-countdown, .bt-lock-card, .bt-task-row (activity with progress and XP), .bt-timeline (lanes), .bt-drawer (the idea panel), plus the Trophy Room's .bt-medal.
