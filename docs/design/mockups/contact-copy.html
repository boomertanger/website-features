Contact reveal and copy

Four ways to show a contact address and copy it, without the lightning. Pick a style, then press Show and Copy on any contact. Reset puts everything back.

Style
Width
Contact

A Pulse and type (recommended)

Show: a soft pulse of light runs down the cable, the ring blooms, and the address types out in a glowing panel with a blinking cursor. Copy: the characters light up left to right, the pulse runs back up the cable, the ring ripples, and a "Copied" tag pops.

B Ticket

Show: a ticket prints out line by line, like a carnival stub, with the address on it. Copy: the ticket tears off and tumbles away, leaving "Copied" behind.

C Blood ink

Show: the address writes itself in dripping blood, letter by letter. Copy: a red "COPIED" stamp slams onto it. The most horror, the hardest to read.

D Envelope

Show: an envelope appears, the flap opens and the letter slides up with the address. Copy: the flap closes and the envelope flies up the cable to the node.

Recommendation: A Pulse and type

It keeps the footer's power story (the same cables carry the signal down and back), the address stays crisp and easy to read or copy by hand, and the "Copied" feedback is unmistakable. It's also calm enough that nobody minds seeing it every time. B is the most fun if you want personality; C is best saved for a spooky special event.
