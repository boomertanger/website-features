4 · /schedule/vote · members vote, visitors see it

Vote for next week

Three blood drops a week. Vote and watch the counts climb, the meters fill and the ranking reshuffle live. The ballot opens seeded with the top Most wanted games and everything Boomer is playing; members can add two more a week. Switch to As a visitor above section 2 to see the join prompt. V1 covers is the default, and the Covers / Race switch lets members flip to V2 (remembered per viewer).

5 · /schedule/plan · owner and admins

The planner

Next week pre-filled from the usual week. The fuse burns toward the close time. Each slot shows its theme, game spots, chats (lime dot covered, gold needed), who can make it and open mod requests. Click a slot, then add games from the tray in priority order: 1 mod requests, 2 community votes, 3 fits the theme, 4 your picks. Covers fly into the slot. Publish week now opens a dialog: what still needs filling, then this week's hero (marquee + frame, or doors + style), then publish. Two ways to show the tray:

★Recommendation: T1, the side tray. The whole week stays in view while you fill it, the four groups read top to bottom in the order you asked for, and mod requests show the mod, their seat and their note. On phones it becomes a bottom sheet. T2 opens the four groups as cover lanes under the slot you picked, which looks great but pushes the rest of the week down.

Decide

Your picks and the new kit pieces

The pills above set your picks. Copy the reply and paste it back in our chat. These pieces would join the UI kit (and the /dev/ui-kit page) instead of living in the feature:

.bt-ticket
A stream as a ticket stub: date stub, perforation, covers that fan out, states for tonight, ended, delayed, cancelled (tape) and backstage (rope).
.bt-marquee.bt-flipclock
The bulb-lit hero frame and the flip-digit countdown. Reusable for any "next up" moment (Contests, Arcade events).
.bt-marquee[data-frame]
One attribute on the marquee. The pool: bulbs, neon, barbed, drip, tape, film, web, electric, vhs, candles, ecg. Seasonal: jack, pumpkin, blizzard, snowman. New frames can be added later without touching the page.
.bt-portal
A day as a portal with five styles (jaws, elevator, coffin, morgue, hinged) and shared states: tonight, ended, cancelled (chained), backstage, day off. Row or carousel ring (.bt-ring).
.bt-view-switch
A small icon switch for a section's view (Tickets / Timeline, Covers / Race), remembered per viewer in localStorage.
.bt-velvet
The members-only chip with a key, in a declared velvet colour. For anything backstage, and later Sub Club.
.bt-vote-card.bt-drops
A cover with a liquid meter and the "votes left" drops. Usable by Chat Games votes too.
.bt-fuse
A deadline line that burns from open to close. Any time-boxed process.
.bt-slot.bt-tray
A slot with game spots, and a grouped picker beside it. Covers fly in (shared/ui/fly.js) and FLIP re-ranking (shared/ui/flip.js, already in the kit).
.bt-tri
Available / Maybe / Can't with a sliding coloured pill.
.bt-poster
A day poster for a repeating routine, with an edit state.
.bt-burst
The small splat burst on votes, seats and publish (respects reduced motion).
