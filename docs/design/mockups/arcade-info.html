How a game is born

From a pitch in the Arcade Studio to a game you can play.

  1. PitchA member with a gamer badge pitches a new game in the Studio.
  2. Community voteMembers vote and discuss. The best pitches rise.
  3. GreenlitBoomertanger picks a pitch to build. It becomes a real game.
  4. WorkshopThe new game gets its own Workshop, and v1 is planned there.
  5. PlaytestMembers try the beta first and report what's broken or boring.
  6. Releasev1 drops in the Arcade, with its own leaderboards.

Versions and patches

You'll always pick a version like v1 or v2. Behind the scenes, fixes are numbered too, so nothing gets lost.

v1

Version

A full release of a game. What you pick to play, and what every leaderboard tracks. New versions add or change real things.

v1.8

Patch

A fix after release: a bug, a sound, a glitch. You'll still see "v1"; the patch number is kept with every run for the record.

v1.4 ⚖

Balance patch

A fix that changes how hard the game is (a faster fuse, a longer window). The board either restarts or shows a note, so times stay fair.

Example:v1released →v1.1 … v1.8bug fixes →v2new version with its own leaderboards

FAQ

Do I need an account to play?

Tap the Splat is free for everyone, as a taste of the Arcade. Every other game is for members: join free to play them, save your runs, get on the leaderboards, vote on ideas and earn badges.

Why doesn't my time show on the leaderboard?

Only finished runs by members count, and each device has its own board. Check you picked the right version and Desktop or Mobile.

How do I become a Keeper?

Keepers are chosen from mods who volunteer when a new version's cycle opens. Keep being helpful in the Workshop; that's how mods get noticed.

What happens to my v1 time when v2 comes out?

It stays on the v1 board forever. v2 gets fresh boards, so everyone starts even.

Can I pitch a game right away?

Pitching unlocks with a gamer badge, like finishing a game. It keeps the Studio full of ideas from people who play.
