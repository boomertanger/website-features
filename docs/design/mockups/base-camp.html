The page

Five chapters, told as a climb that starts at camp. H1 is set. New this round: chapter 2 is a hand of three playing cards (Aces of Hearts, Ghosts and Gears) that turn over, and the Top 5 stage cards are rebuilt from scratch. Hover the stage cards: each one has a small idle motion to draw you in and a bigger moment on hover. On phones they play when they scroll into view. The placard stays as a fallback to compare.

★What changed in the stage cards. Each card now has a glowing ring that runs around its edge, a 3D tilt that follows the pointer, a soft spotlight, a big outlined number, and a scene with depth (light, shadow, layers). 1 The hallway light flickers; on hover it dies, the door creaks open, red eyes appear and the scream meter jumps. 2 Dust hangs in a spotlight; on hover one game slides off the shelf. 3 Chat is typing; on hover the messages flood in and the vote swings left. 4 The shield catches the light and the crew rises with stars. 5 The fans spin up and a pulse runs from the gaming PC to the streaming PC.
