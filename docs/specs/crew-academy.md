## 1. Welcome to the crew
`welcome` · 4 min · Required for Initiate

**Hero:** You're here because people trust you in chat. This is how we keep that trust.

**Ch 1 · What the crew is for.** The crew keeps every chat welcoming, fun and safe while Boomer plays, on Twitch, both YouTube chats and TikTok. Most of the job is being friendly. A tiny part is stepping in when someone isn't.

**Ch 2 · Life comes first.** You're a volunteer. Two duties a month keeps you Active (three for Wardens and Sentinels). Step away whenever you need to; hand off if it'll be a while. Plan a break with Going dark. Nobody here is guilted for having a life.

**Ch 3 · The Crew Code** (placard, the six rules from How it works): Life comes first · Friendly before firm · Personal info stays private · Mod powers aren't for grudges · Flag what matters · We're one crew.

**Ch 4 · Who to ask.** Your Room Lead on a stream, the Captain for anything that touches more than one chat, Boomer for anything serious (through Flag in the Mod Deck, never a DM mid-stream).

**Try it:** the Crew Code placard has a "Which rule is this?" card: three short chat moments; tap the rule each one tests.

## 2. House rules and the escalation ladder
`house-rules` · 4 min · Required for Initiate · (layout shown in the batch 2 mockup)

**Hero:** Most of the job is being friendly. When someone breaks a rule, go up the ladder one step at a time.

**Ch 1 · Why it matters.** Most people just didn't know · Everyone is watching how you handle one person · Boomer shouldn't have to stop the game to moderate.

**Ch 2 · The ladder** (placard): 01 Remind (a light public nudge) · 02 Warn (name the rule and what's next; calm, short, never sarcastic) · 03 Timeout (1 to 10 minutes; note it in the Mod Deck) · 04 Ban (won't stop, or anything in chapter 3).

**Ch 3 · Skip straight to a ban:** hate or slurs · threats or doxxing · spam bots and scam links. Then flag the Captain and Boomer. **Someone in crisis is different:** never ban; flag Boomer right away (Safety module).

**Ch 4 · Never:** argue in chat · repeat or screenshot personal info · use mod powers on a friend's say-so · DM a viewer who's under 18.

**House rules for chat** (what we remind people of; confirmed Oct 6): be kind · no spoilers for games Boomer hasn't finished · no backseating unless he asks · no self-promo or links · English only, so every mod can read it · keep it PG-13 in words, horror in spirit.

**Try it:** the quiz (pick an answer, BOOMBOT replies).

**Quiz**
1. A new viewer posts a link to their own stream. First step?
   - Ban · ✓ Remind them self-promo isn't allowed · 10-minute timeout
   - "Start with the gentlest step that works. Most people just didn't know."
2. Someone keeps spoiling the ending after a reminder and a warning. Next?
   - Another reminder · ✓ A short timeout · A ban
   - "Third time is a timeout. Note it in the Mod Deck so the next lead knows."
3. Which of these skips straight to a ban?
   - Backseating · Posting in another language (a reminder: English only) · ✓ A slur aimed at another viewer
   - "Hate gets no reminder. Ban, then flag."
4. A viewer says they don't want to be here anymore. You:
   - Ban, it's against the rules · Ignore it, it's probably a joke · ✓ Don't ban; flag Boomer right away
   - "Someone in crisis is never a moderation problem. Flag it now; the Safety module covers what to say."
5. Someone argues with your warning. Best response?
   - Explain the rule in detail until they agree · ✓ Stay short and calm; if it continues, timeout · Argue back so chat sees you're right
   - "Never argue in chat. Short, calm, then the next step."

## 3. Platform guides
`platforms` · 7 min · Required for Initiate

**Hero:** Four chats, three apps, one crew. Here's where the buttons are.

**Ch 1 · Twitch.** Mod tools from any message (click a name): delete, timeout, ban. Commands work too: `/timeout name 300`, `/ban name`, `/unban name`. Mod View gives you the chat, the activity feed and AutoMod's held messages in one place. AutoMod holds risky messages for you to approve or deny. Shield Mode exists for raids; only the Captain switches it on.

**Ch 2 · YouTube (two chats).** When Boomer streams on YouTube from his phone there are two broadcasts, landscape and vertical, each with its own chat. You're added as a moderator of each broadcast when the stream starts (Mod Machina does this for scheduled leads). From a message's menu: remove, put user in timeout, hide user on the channel. "Held for review" messages need a yes or no. The Mod Deck shows both YouTube chats side by side; on your phone, use the YouTube app on the vertical stream.

**Ch 3 · TikTok.** TikTok LIVE moderators are added by Boomer in the app (Mod Machina keeps a checklist; there's no way to do it automatically). Long-press a comment to mute (choose a length), block or report. Keyword filters catch the worst. TikTok has no chat feed the site can show, so the Deck links out.

**Ch 4 · One crew, every chat.** Use the same ladder everywhere, even where the buttons differ. Post your chat's check-in code when you open, and every 20 minutes or so. Nudge people toward the other chats when a Chat Game clue drops there.

**Try it:** a "Where's the button?" picker: choose a platform and an action (timeout, remove, hide, approve held message), and it highlights where to tap in a drawn mini chat for that app.

**Quiz**
1. How many chats does a YouTube mobile stream have?
   - One · ✓ Two, landscape and vertical · Three
   - "Two broadcasts, two chats. One lead can cover both from the Deck early on."
2. Where do Twitch's held messages show up?
   - Nowhere, they're deleted · ✓ AutoMod, in Mod View · Boomer's DMs
   - "AutoMod holds them for a mod to approve or deny."
3. Who turns on Twitch Shield Mode during a raid?
   - Any mod · ✓ The Captain · Boomer only
   - "It changes the whole chat, so it's the Captain's call."
4. How do you become a TikTok LIVE moderator?
   - Mod Machina adds you automatically · ✓ Boomer adds you in the TikTok app · You request it in chat
   - "TikTok has no way to do it from the site, so it's on Boomer's checklist."
5. A Chat Game clue just dropped in YouTube vertical, and you lead Twitch. You:
   - Post the clue in Twitch too · ✓ Tell Twitch chat a clue just dropped in YouTube vertical · Say nothing
   - "Never repost clues. Point people to the chat that has it; that's the game."

## 4. Stream duty
`stream-duty` · 5 min · Required for Initiate

**Hero:** Take a seat, clock in, step away when you need to. Here's how a stream works from the crew's side.

**Ch 1 · Seats** (flow, from How it works): Deckhand → Room Lead → Stream Captain → Boomer. Watchers can lead a chat; Wardens can Captain.

**Ch 2 · Signing up.** Take a seat from the schedule or your HQ. Taking one 48 hours ahead earns +3 Gears. Reminders come 24 h and 1 h before.

**Ch 3 · On the night.** Open the Mod Deck, clock in (within 15 minutes of the start for the +5), post your chat's check-in code, welcome people, follow the cues. Drop-ins are welcome any time we're live.

**Ch 4 · Pass the lantern.** Step away for 5, 15 or 30 minutes, or for the night. Deckhands are asked to take the lead; the first to accept carries it; Gears follow the minutes. No one in your chat? The Captain is pinged.

**Ch 5 · Can't make it?** Drop the seat on the swap board 24 hours ahead and nothing counts against you. Three no-shows in 90 days without dropping pauses Lead and Captain seats for 30 days.

**Try it:** Pass the lantern (the working handoff example from How it works).

**Quiz**
1. You need 10 minutes away mid-stream. What do you press?

   - "Step away asks a Deckhand to take the lead and hands it back when you return."
2. When should you drop a seat you can't make?
   - Right before the stream · ✓ At least 24 hours ahead, on the swap board · Never, just don't show
   - "24 hours ahead costs you nothing. No-shows add up."
3. You open the Deck 20 minutes after the start. Do you get the +5 for showing up?
   - ✓ No, that's for clocking in within 15 minutes · Yes, always · Only on YouTube
   - "You still earn your hourly Gears; only the on-time bonus is missed."
4. Who becomes Captain if the Captain steps away?

## 5. Engagement playbook
`engagement` · 6 min · Required for Watcher

**Hero:** A quiet chat isn't a moderation problem. It's an invitation.

**Ch 1 · Welcome every new name.** The Deck highlights first messages. A name and a hello beats a bot greeting every time: "welcome in Rachel! he's on night 3, chat thinks the doctor is behind the curtain."

**Ch 2 · Give chat something to answer.** Predictions ("door or vent?"), polls by emoji, "first time watching Outlast?", call-backs to last stream. One prompt every 10 to 15 quiet minutes, not a stream of them.

**Ch 3 · Bridge the chats.** "Clue 3 just dropped in YouTube vertical" · "TikTok is winning Scream Off per viewer, Twitch, you good?" · the check-in code. Never repost content from one chat to another.

**Ch 4 · Read the room.** Big scare coming? Go quiet and let chat react. Boomer reading chat? Don't stack messages on top of his.

**Ch 5 · Quick lines** (from the Deck): house rules, socials, schedule, your recruit link. Copy, paste, done.

**Try it:** "Revive this chat": a quiet example chat with three suggested prompts; pick one and watch the example chat answer.

**Quiz**
1. A first-time chatter says hi. Best reply?
   - "!welcome" · ✓ Their name and a hello with a little context · Nothing, Boomer will see it
   - "A name and a real hello is what makes people come back."
2. Chat has been quiet for 15 minutes. You:
   - Post five prompts in a row · ✓ Post one question chat can answer · Wait for Boomer
   - "One good prompt beats a wall of them."
3. A big scare is clearly coming. You:
   - ✓ Go quiet and let chat react · Post a prediction poll · Remind the rules
   - "Let the moment land."
4. How do you send people to another chat for a Chat Game clue?
   - Repost the clue · ✓ Say where it dropped · DM people the clue
   - "Pointing is the game. Reposting spoils it."
5. Where do your quick lines live?
   - In a notes app · ✓ In the Mod Deck, one tap to copy · In Discord
   - "The Deck keeps your code, rules, socials and link ready to paste."

## 8. Hosting Chat Games
`chat-games` · 6 min · Unlocks with Chat Games (marked Soon until then)

**Hero:** Members play, the crew hosts. Here's how to run Dead Air, Scream Off, Scare Bingo and Body Count.

**Ch 1 · The pool:** 3 votes a week; "I'll host this" counts three times. Pledge only what you'll run.

**Ch 2 · Dead Air:** each clue goes to one chat. When your cue is due, post it exactly as written, press Posted, then hype it. Never repost clues from other chats; point people to them. Inside Job nights: if you're cast as the culprit, slip one tell per clue, and never tell anyone.

**Ch 3 · Scream Off:** post your chat's code, hype check-ins; scores are per viewer, so small chats can win.

**Ch 4 · Scare Bingo and Body Count:** call squares only when they really happen; the Captain can undo. Tap counters honestly.

**Try it:** a cue drill: a clue comes due on a timer; post it, press Posted, write the hype line.

## 9. Safety
`safety` · 8 min · Required for Warden

**Hero:** The rare moments that matter most. What to do, what to say, and when to flag Boomer.

**Ch 1 · Personal info.** Remove it immediately. Never repeat, screenshot or share it, even to report it. Flag the Captain and Boomer.

**Ch 2 · Minors in chat.** Many viewers are 13 to 17. Never DM a viewer who's under 18 (or might be). Never ask anyone's age, school or location. If an adult is pressing a young viewer for private contact, remove, timeout and flag.

**Ch 3 · Harassment and raids.** Hate, threats and pile-ons: ban and flag. Raids: tell the Captain at once; the Captain decides on slow mode, followers-only, Shield Mode (Twitch) or held-for-review settings. Don't engage the raiders.

**Ch 4 · Someone in crisis.** If someone says they want to hurt themselves or aren't safe: don't ban, don't argue, don't play therapist. Reply once, kindly, in chat: "Hey, we see you and we're glad you're here. If you're in the US you can call or text 988 any time. Boomer's being told." Then flag Boomer right away. Outside the US, the Captain posts the right local line. Treat it as real every time.

**Ch 5 · Taking care of yourself.** It's OK to step away after something heavy. Tell the Captain, pass the lantern.

**Try it:** a "Flag or handle?" sorter: eight short chat moments; sort each into Handle with the ladder, Ban and flag, or Flag Boomer now.

**Quiz**
1. Someone posts a viewer's phone number. First step? · ✓ Remove it, then flag · Screenshot it as evidence · Ask them to delete it
2. A 15-year-old viewer asks you to DM them. You · DM them · ✓ Kindly say crew don't DM viewers and keep it in chat · Ban them
3. A raid floods Twitch chat with slurs. Who decides on Shield Mode? · You · ✓ The Captain · Nobody
4. Someone writes that they don't want to live anymore. You · ban · ignore it as a joke · ✓ reply kindly with 988, then flag Boomer now
5. After a heavy moment you need a break. You · stay anyway · ✓ tell the Captain and pass the lantern · clock out without telling anyone

## 10. Captain's course
`captain` · 10 min · Required for Stream Captain

**Hero:** One person watches every chat, so everyone else can watch theirs.

**Ch 1 · Before the stream:** check the seats (gold chats need someone; ask drop-ins), confirm the Chat Game cues, know who's new.

**Ch 2 · Coverage:** the chat tiles. A gold tile is your job: cover it, move a seat or ask a Deckhand to step up.

**Ch 3 · Live drops:** drop a badge from Captain tools; windows from the badge's preset (rewards.md §10a); one per badge per stream.

**Ch 4 · Incidents:** raids (Shield Mode, slow mode, followers-only), personal info, crisis messages. Flag Boomer with one clear sentence; he reads it between moments, not mid-scare.

**Ch 5 · After:** a two-line wrap-up in crew notes (what went well, anything to follow up), rate the Chat Game.

**Try it:** a coverage drill: a live example of the four chat tiles where leads step away on a timer; keep every tile green.

**Quiz**
1. TikTok's tile turns gold mid-stream. You · ignore it · ✓ cover it or move a seat · end the stream
2. How many times can one badge drop per stream? · ✓ Once · Twice · Unlimited
3. A raid hits. First call? · ✓ Shield Mode / slow mode on the raided chat · ban everyone · tell chat to ignore it
4. Flagging Boomer looks like · a paragraph · ✓ one clear sentence · a DM
5. After the stream, the Captain · ✓ writes a two-line wrap-up and rates the game · nothing · posts the Gears
