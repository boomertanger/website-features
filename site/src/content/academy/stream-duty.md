## Ch 1 · Seats

Every chat on a stream has a seat for a lead, with Deckhands helping and the Stream Captain watching them all. Watchers can lead a chat; Wardens can Captain.

## Ch 2 · Signing up

Take a seat from the schedule or your HQ. Taking one 48 hours ahead earns +3 Gears. Reminders come 24 h and 1 h before.

## Ch 3 · On the night

Open the Mod Deck, clock in (within 15 minutes of the start for the +5), post your chat's check-in code, welcome people, follow the cues. Drop-ins are welcome any time we're live.

## Ch 4 · Pass the lantern

Step away for 5, 15 or 30 minutes, or for the night. Deckhands are asked to take the lead; the first to accept carries it; Gears follow the minutes. No one in your chat? The Captain is pinged.

## Ch 5 · Can't make it?

Drop the seat on the swap board 24 hours ahead and nothing counts against you. Three no-shows in 90 days without dropping pauses Lead and Captain seats for 30 days.
