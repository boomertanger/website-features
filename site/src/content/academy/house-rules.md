## Ch 1 · Why it matters

A chat that sees mods being fair stays a chat people want to be in.

## Ch 2 · The ladder

Four steps. Go up one at a time, starting with the gentlest step that works.

## Ch 3 · Skip straight to a ban

Some things don't get a reminder. Ban, then flag the Captain and Boomer. **Someone in crisis is different:** never ban; flag Boomer right away (the Safety module covers what to say).
