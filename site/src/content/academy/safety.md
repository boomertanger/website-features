## Ch 1 · Personal info

Remove it immediately. Never repeat, screenshot or share it, even to report it. Flag the Captain and Boomer.

## Ch 2 · Minors in chat

Many viewers are 13 to 17. Never DM a viewer who's under 18 (or might be). Never ask anyone's age, school or location. If an adult is pressing a young viewer for private contact, remove, timeout and flag.

## Ch 3 · Harassment and raids

Hate, threats and pile-ons: ban and flag. Raids: tell the Captain at once; the Captain decides on slow mode, followers-only, Shield Mode (Twitch) or held-for-review settings. Don't engage the raiders.

## Ch 4 · Someone in crisis

If someone says they want to hurt themselves or aren't safe: don't ban, don't argue, don't play therapist. Reply once, kindly, in chat: "Hey, we see you and we're glad you're here. If you're in the US you can call or text 988 any time. Boomer's being told." Then flag Boomer right away. Outside the US, the Captain posts the right local line. Treat it as real every time.

## Ch 5 · Taking care of yourself

It's OK to step away after something heavy. Tell the Captain, pass the lantern.
