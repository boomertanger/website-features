## Ch 1 · The pool

Members get 3 votes a week, and "I'll host this" counts three times. Pledge only what you'll run.

## Ch 2 · Dead Air

Each clue goes to one chat. When your cue is due, post it exactly as written, press Posted, then hype it. Never repost clues from other chats; point people to them. Inside Job nights: if you're cast as the culprit, slip one tell per clue, and never tell anyone.

## Ch 3 · Scream Off

Post your chat's code and hype check-ins. Scores are per viewer, so small chats can win.

## Ch 4 · Scare Bingo and Body Count

Call squares only when they really happen; the Captain can undo. Tap counters honestly.
