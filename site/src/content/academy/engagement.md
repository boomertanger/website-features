## Ch 1 · Welcome every new name

The Deck highlights first messages. A name and a hello beats a bot greeting every time.

## Ch 2 · Give chat something to answer

Give chat a question it can answer: a prediction, an emoji poll, a first-timers question or a call-back to last stream. One prompt every 10 to 15 quiet minutes, not a stream of them.

## Ch 3 · Bridge the chats

Point people to the other chats with a line that says where things are happening, plus your chat's check-in code. Never repost content from one chat to another.

## Ch 4 · Read the room

Big scare coming? Go quiet and let chat react. Boomer reading chat? Don't stack messages on top of his.

## Ch 5 · Quick lines

The Mod Deck keeps quick lines ready: house rules, socials, schedule and your recruit link. Copy, paste, done.
