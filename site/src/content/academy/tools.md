## Ch 1 · Mod Deck

The Mod Deck is where a stream happens for you:

- The bar: the clock, your seat and Clock out
- Chat tiles, one for every chat
- The chat wall, with a Focus toggle for one chat at a time
- Your seat and Step away
- The Chat Game cue
- Quick lines
- Flag the Captain

## Ch 2 · Crew HQ

Crew HQ is home base between streams:

- Your time card
- Duties, and Swap
- Open seats
- Your next grade
- The task board
- Your recruit link

## Ch 3 · Task board

Claim a task, do it, mark it done. Someone else confirms before Gears pay.

## Ch 4 · Gears

Gears are how the crew counts help. HQ shows what earns them (and the things that earn zero), the 1.5× YouTube boost, and a worked example of one night's total.

## Ch 5 · Awarding badges

Watchers can award up to Uncommon, Wardens up to Rare, always with a written reason, never to yourself, with a daily cap.
