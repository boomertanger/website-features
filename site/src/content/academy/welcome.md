## Ch 1 · What the crew is for

The crew keeps every chat welcoming, fun and safe while Boomer plays, on Twitch, both YouTube chats and TikTok. Most of the job is being friendly. A tiny part is stepping in when someone isn't.

## Ch 2 · Life comes first

You're a volunteer. Two duties a month keeps you Active (three for Wardens and Sentinels). Step away whenever you need to; hand off if it'll be a while. Plan a break with Going dark. Nobody here is guilted for having a life.

## Ch 3 · The Crew Code

Six rules every crew member agrees to. Read them once, then keep them close.

## Ch 4 · Who to ask

Your Room Lead on a stream, the Captain for anything that touches more than one chat, Boomer for anything serious (through Flag in the Mod Deck, never a DM mid-stream).
