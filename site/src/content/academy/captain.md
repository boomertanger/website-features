## Ch 1 · Before the stream

Check the seats (gold chats need someone; ask drop-ins), confirm the Chat Game cues, and know who's new.

## Ch 2 · Coverage

The chat tiles show coverage. A gold tile is your job: cover it, move a seat or ask a Deckhand to step up.

## Ch 3 · Live drops

Drop a badge from Captain tools. Windows come from the badge's preset, and each badge drops once per stream.

## Ch 4 · Incidents

Raids (Shield Mode, slow mode, followers-only), personal info and crisis messages. Flag Boomer with one clear sentence; he reads it between moments, not mid-scare.

## Ch 5 · After

Write a two-line wrap-up in crew notes (what went well, anything to follow up) and rate the Chat Game.
