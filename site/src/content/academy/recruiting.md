## Ch 1 · Your link

`boomertanger.com/join/@you`. It counts when the person signs up and is still active after 7 days. Crew earn 10 Gears per recruit (up to 10 a month); everyone earns the Recruiter badges.

## Ch 2 · Where it's welcome

**Where it's welcome:** your own socials, Discord servers that allow it, and friends who'd enjoy it.

**Where it isn't:** DMs to strangers, other streamers' chats, and more than once an hour in our chats.

## Ch 3 · Recruit Rush

Some nights have a goal bar for new members; hitting it unlocks something for everyone. Hype the goal, not your link.
