# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [effective date]**

This policy explains what information Boomertanger LLC ("Boomertanger", "we", "us") collects when you use boomertanger.com and its related pages (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email support@boomertanger.com.

## What we collect

**When you just visit.** You can browse the Site and play its public games without an account. Our hosting provider records standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to deliver the Site and keep it secure. Your browser may also store small settings on your device, like your sound preference in a game.

**When you create an account.** We collect:

- Your email address, and the sign-in method you chose (Google, Twitch or email).
- The handle and display name you pick.
- Your birth month and year, used only to confirm you are 13 or older. We store them privately and never show them.
- The date you agreed to these terms and the privacy choices you set.

**When you connect another account.** If you sign in with or link Google or Twitch, we receive your account ID on that service, and your email address if that service shares it. We don't receive your password for those services.

**When you take part.** We store what you create or do on the Site, such as game runs and times, votes, comments, ideas, bug reports and uploaded screenshots.

**Public statistics.** We collect Boomertanger's own public follower counts from Twitch, YouTube and TikTok to show them on the Site. This doesn't involve your data.

## How we use it

- To run your account and the Site's features, like leaderboards, voting and comments.
- To show your handle, display name and public activity (for example, a leaderboard time or a comment) to other visitors.
- To send emails you need, such as sign-in links, email verification and password resets, and stream reminders only if you turn them on.
- To keep the Site safe: preventing spam, cheating and abuse, and enforcing our Terms.
- To understand how features are used, so we can improve them.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.

## Who we share it with

We use a few service providers to run the Site. They process information only on our behalf:

- **Google Firebase** (accounts, database, and server functions).
- **Cloudflare** (hosting and security).
- **Cloudinary** (storing uploaded images, such as bug report screenshots).
- **Google and Twitch**, when you choose to sign in with them.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or as part of a business transfer, such as a merger.

## What others can see

Your handle, display name, avatar and public activity are visible to anyone. Your email address, birth month and year, and linked account IDs are never shown publicly. Linked accounts appear on your profile only if you turn that on.

## Your choices and rights

- **Edit** your handle, display name and privacy settings on your account page at any time.
- **Unlink** Google or Twitch from your account page.
- **Turn off** stream reminder emails at any time.
- **Get a copy of your data or delete your account:** these tools are coming to your account page. Until then, email support@boomertanger.com from the address on your account and we'll handle it within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights, such as asking what we hold about you or asking us to correct it. Email us and we'll help.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we remove your personal information within 30 days, except where we must keep something for legal, security or fraud reasons. Anonymous game statistics may remain.

## Children

The Site is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email support@boomertanger.com and we'll delete it.

## Security

We protect information with access controls, encrypted connections, and by keeping sensitive data on our servers only. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password if you sign in with email.

## Changes

We'll post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll let members know by email or on the Site.
